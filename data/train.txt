The dawn opens softly over the river.
Quiet stars remember the names of rain.
New winds carry seeds toward a patient field.
The moon listens beneath the cedar branches.
Bright birds cross the wide blue homeward sky.
Kind hands shape tomorrow from ordinary clay.
Inward fires awaken when the morning calls.
Green leaves greet the rain and hold its light.
We learn from silence and return with hope.
Old stones hold stories beneath the moss.
Clear water finds the sea beyond the hill.
Morning carries a small golden promise.
The patient heart keeps walking through winter.
Light enters every room that welcomes it.
The river gathers clouds and gives them back.
Across the quiet earth, new gardens rise.
